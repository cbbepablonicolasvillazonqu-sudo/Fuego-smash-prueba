Why it tastes different

It's not a big burger: it's a burger done right. The difference comes down to three things.

Flavor you can taste

Beef pressed against a hot griddle: seared outside, juicy inside. That's what smash means.

It comes out fast

A thin patty cooks in minutes. You order, wait a little and eat it hot, the way it should be.

Built in front of you

You see the griddle, hear the sizzle and get it handed to you. Nothing leaves a kitchen you can't watch.

Questions we get all the time

Do you deliver?

Yes, on Uber Eats — look for Fuego Smash. That said, if you're nearby, come in: a smash burger is at its best in the first few minutes, straight off the griddle.

Do I need a reservation?

No need. Come in, order at the counter and grab a seat.

How much does it cost to eat here?

The Double Smash Clásica is $12.99 and the BBQ Bacon Smash is $14.49. With a side and a drink, figure $10 to $20 per person. All prices are in the menu above.

Do you have vegetarian or gluten-free options?

Yes. We have a vegetarian option, and gluten-free on request — just tell us when you order. If you have an allergy, let us know and we'll walk you through how everything is cooked.

How can I pay?

We take cash, debit and credit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay.
